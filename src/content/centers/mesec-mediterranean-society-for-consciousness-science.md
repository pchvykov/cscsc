Grassroots association of researchers in training in consciousness science, founded 2021 out of a workshop in Campomoro, Corsica. Grown with support from two Templeton World Charity Foundation grants. Horizontal structure: executive board, event organization teams, task forces; alumni ("MESECeers") join by attending an event.

Stated core values: intellectual curiosity and humility, inclusive empowerment of early-career researchers, and "shared qualia" - the sun and the sea, on the premise that enjoying experiences together nurtures scientific partnership. A standing rule that events be held somewhere beautiful, near the sea.

**Why it is in this database**: surfaced 2026-09-16 by Peter Thestrup Waade (ETH Zürich, and on the MESEC forum) as a working model for how CSCSC might become a self-sustaining community - see the vault note `260916-mesec-as-a-community-template-for-cscsc`. The mechanisms most directly portable are the conference "Harbor" (block-booked shared lodging at events someone else runs), the alumni residency, the member geography directory, and the transition from a small core team to rotating organizers.
