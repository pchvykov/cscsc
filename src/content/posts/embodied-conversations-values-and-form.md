## The Aim

We are essentially just trying to experiment with different methods of inquiry; by ‘different’ we mean different than some traditional academic forms or atmospheres many of us have been exposed to, which can sometimes feel too uptight, rigid, or clenched into certainty. The aim then is not to be right, or to arrive at the right opinion, but to move through something together and attend to what it opens. In this respect, it is also not just about ‘thinking differently’ but about ‘doing things differently’.

Keeping this in mind, the form is deliberately flexible. Below we have provided an anchor schedule (which is more or less a reading group that focuses on one article) but if people would like to experiment with their own formats and ways of doing dialogue this is welcome.

## Values

Three values that guide the sessions:

**Intuition** Follow the hunch, the thing that strikes you before you can justify it. Feeling comes first, and we would like to have people voice whatever subtlety is emerging in them.

**Care** The session is something we are *in* together, not a series of individual performances. Care means listening as much as we speak, building on what others offer, and feeling for the whole rather than only our own thread. Related to this, basic kindness and respect for others is absolutely required.

**Curiosity** Curiosity keeps us both humble and open to learning new things.

## Anchor Schedule

Since people are free to suggest their own practices and methods for inquiry, we have this as an anchor schedule but people are more than welcome to reach out and propose new formats to switch things up.

| Time | | Aim | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **11:00 – 11:05** | *Coming together* | Arriving, settling, letting everyone land in the space. | Hello! |
| **11:05 – 11:10** | *Settling practice* | A brief meditation to drop out of the day and into the body. | Guided meditation or practice from someone in the group |
| **11:10 – 11:20** | *Check-in* | Where we're coming from, how we're feeling and becoming present to one another. More so life-related things. | Popcorn style; sometimes a prompt, other times just saying how we are feeling |
| **11:20 – 11:25** | *Introducing the article/symbol* | Whoever chose the piece says a few words on what they brought and why. | Just the person who chose the article |
| **11:25 – 11:35** | *Brief initial comments* | Everyone provides initial a reflections on what stands out | Popcorn style; 1 min max, 1 word min |
| **11:35 – 11:45** | *Breakout pairs* | Splitting off to go deeper with one or two others. | Breakout rooms; discuss as you wish |
| **11:45 – 11:55** | *Sharing pearls* | Back in the full circle, identifying threads the came up | Flowing style (everybody shares one pearl, no specific order) |
| **11:55 – 12:20** | *Group discussion* | Open conversation following what's alive, then a brief closing round to land the session. | Flowing style (anyone can speak at any time) |
| **12:20- 12:30** | *Check-out* | Reflecting on the session and how we’re leaving. | Popcorn style |

## Themes We're Drawn To

Any contributions are welcome here, but a few currents run through this community and are especially alive for us to explore:

- **Ontology and the ontological turn** — questions of what is taken to be real, and how different worlds and ways of being hang together.
- **Contemplative knowing** — knowledge arrived at through attention, practice, and direct experience rather than analysis alone.
- **Geophilosophy** — thinking with the earth, place, and the more-than-human; philosophy rooted in territory rather than abstraction.
- **New ways of doing research and science** — modes of inquiry that make room for animist and phenomenological knowing alongside the empirical, and that take lived, embodied experience seriously as a source of understanding.
- **Institutionalizing new ways of knowing** — how these modes might be held, supported, and grown inside (or alongside) existing structures, rather than remaining marginal.
- **Social imaginaries** — the shared, often unspoken pictures of the world that organize how we live, and how they might be sensed, questioned, and re-formed.
