A theoretical physicist (PhD, MIT) who works on how matter organizes itself without anyone in charge. In his "low rattling" work (*Science*, 2021), a swarm of simple robots kept settling into the arrangements where they jiggled least - a principle that lets the statistics of noise predict which kind of order appears. After a stint as an AI scientist at General Motors he started asking the same question about minds: his current project models mindfulness with predictive coding. He founded CSCSC in 2025 and designs its retreats.
