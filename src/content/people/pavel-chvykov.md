Pavel trained as a theoretical physicist (PhD, MIT), studying how order arises in systems that no one is steering. Alongside that work he spent years in contemplative and relational practice, and kept finding that the two were looking at the same thing from opposite sides: how a mind, a body or a group organizes itself. Academia had room for one or the other, rarely both in the same conversation, so in 2025 he started CSCSC to make that room. He works on both directions of the exchange: what the formal tools of complexity science can say about questions contemplatives care about, and what contemplative practice can change in how research itself is done.
