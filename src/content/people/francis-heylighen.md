Francis is a cyberneticist at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel, where he leads the Center Leo Apostel for interdisciplinary research (CLEA). Since co-founding the Principia Cybernetica Project in 1989 he has worked toward one vocabulary for self-organization, from networks of chemical reactions to the "global brain" of a connected society. In his process view, agents and minds are networks of interactions rather than things - close to the "relational and emergent" reality that CSCSC starts from.
