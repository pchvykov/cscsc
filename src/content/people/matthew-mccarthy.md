Matthew studies collective imaginaries at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel: the shared pictures of the world a group lives inside, mostly without noticing. His interests run from the ontological turn in anthropology and geophilosophy (thinking with land and place) to the question of how new ways of knowing can be held inside institutions rather than staying at the margins. His question for CSCSC is the one a young field meets first: how people trained in physics, ecology, philosophy and contemplative traditions build a vocabulary they can share.
