Matthew studies collective imaginaries at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel - the shared pictures of the world a group lives inside, mostly without noticing. He writes most of the posts on this site, runs the reading group and the monthly email, and keeps the forum going. His question for CSCSC is the one a young field meets first: how people trained in physics, ecology, philosophy and contemplative traditions build a vocabulary they can share.
