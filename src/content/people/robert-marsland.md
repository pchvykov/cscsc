Bobby spent his physics career on large, diverse ecosystems - what the statistics of thousands of interacting species say about which of them can coexist - most recently as a postdoc at Boston University. He is now a Catholic priest and a doctoral student in theology at the Pontifical University of the Holy Cross in Rome. He co-founded CSCSC, and holds the end of the conversation where a physicist's model of a living system and a contemplative tradition's account of it have to meet without either being reduced to the other.
