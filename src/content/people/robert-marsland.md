Fr. Robert did his PhD in physics at MIT, on large, diverse ecosystems: what the statistics of thousands of interacting species say about which of them can coexist. After a postdoc, he went on to a second doctorate, in theology at the Pontifical University of the Holy Cross in Rome, and became a Catholic priest. Holding both ways of knowing in one life, he co-founded CSCSC to bring them into one conversation, and he holds the place where a physicist's model of a living system and a contemplative tradition's account of it have to meet without either being reduced to the other.
