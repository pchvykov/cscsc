John holds the Distinguished Chair in Contemplative Humanities at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, where he works with the Center for Healthy Minds. A scholar of Buddhist philosophy - in particular Indian and Tibetan accounts of perception and knowledge - he has spent much of his career putting those accounts in conversation with cognitive science: in studies of consciousness, of intensive meditation practice, and of contemplative education. He is a Fellow of the Mind & Life Institute and has served on its board. His work is the closest precedent for what we are attempting - contemplative theory treated as a partner in science, and not only as its object.
