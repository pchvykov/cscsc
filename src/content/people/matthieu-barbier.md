Matthieu is a theoretical ecologist at the Plant Health Institute in Montpellier (CIRAD), who uses statistical mechanics to ask what large food webs and plant microbiomes can and cannot be predicted to do. To CSCSC he brings epistemic mapping: understanding how different disciplines think and what moves the people in them, so that work across disciplines can actually be productive. That is also what he is building at INTP, the research residence he directs in the French Pyrenees, where our retreats take place: a place where researchers from different fields work together on more than their methods. It sits close to our idea of a research monastery.
