Matthieu is a theoretical ecologist at the Plant Health Institute in Montpellier (CIRAD), who uses statistical mechanics to ask what large food webs and plant microbiomes can and cannot be predicted to do. He directs INTP, a research residence in the French Pyrenees that hosts our retreats. In CSCSC he led the Telics working group, on how to state goal-directed explanations ("the cell does this in order to...") in a language precise enough to test.
