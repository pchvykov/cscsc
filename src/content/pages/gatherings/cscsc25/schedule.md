#### **9:20AM: Keynote - Gary Lupyan "**How language helps structure the human mind**"**

Does language merely reflect objective categories of our mental world, or are they (in part) the creations of language itself? On one widespread view learning a language involves mapping words onto pre-existing concepts. Alternatively, many of our concepts — including some that seem very basic — may derive from our experience with and use of language. I will argue in favor of this second view, and focus on two ways in which languages transmits semantic knowledge: (1) through its vocabulary, and (2) through the statistical patterns in language. I will draw on a behavioral experiments and work with large language models to argue that language plays a key role in structuring the human mind and may be a necessary ingredient for the emergence of human-like intelligence.
