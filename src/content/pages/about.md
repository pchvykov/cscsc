*A research community bridging complexity science and contemplative studies to understand minds, societies, and human flourishing*

## Welcome to the Complexity Science and Contemplative Studies Community

We are an emerging research community at the intersection of complexity science and contemplative studies, united by the vision that rigorous scientific inquiry and contemplative wisdom can illuminate each other in profound ways.

---

<aside class="callout bg-blue">
<span class="icon" aria-hidden="true">🎯</span>
<div class="body">

### Our Mission

Acknowledging the long history and challenges of attempts to formalize contemplative philosophy as a hard science, we propose that the young field of complexity science provides new opportunities for this age-old quest.

**Our intention is three-fold:**

- **Foster community** among experts working on either side of this intersection
- **Collectively define** the core questions and opportunities of this space
- **Embody the ideals** of contemplation and scientific rigor in our work

</div>
</aside>

### Our Approach

We navigate between two essential polarities:

**Contemplative ⟷ Scientific**

We honor both subjective, first-person contemplative experience and objective, third-person scientific observation, seeking their productive synthesis rather than reduction of one to the other.

**Theoretical ⟷ Applied**

We pursue rigorous mathematical and computational frameworks while remaining grounded in meaningful applications that address real human needs and global challenges.

</div>
</aside>

### Our DNA: Ideas We Agree On

**Core ideas that identify our community:**

- Reality is fundamentally **relational and emergent**, not built from isolated parts
  - Any parts are **adaptive and intelligent**, not inert mechanisms
- Rigor requires integrating the **first-, second-, and third-person perspectives:**
  - **1st:** individual introspection, felt-sense truth (e.g., 1+1=2)
  - **2nd:** relational progress, like scientific discourse (connect and transform)
  - **3rd:** objective reproducible results (scientific method)
- **Science as a contemplative tradition**, striving for discovery (expansion) over closure
  - We hold generative tensions between polarities (e.g., knowing vs emptiness) instead of seeking easy resolution
  - Awe or felt sense as guidance for direction or truth
  - Complexity as humility, realizing that closure isn't possible, allowing for openness or epistemic provision
- **Open non-sectarianism:** balance among various contemplative and scientific traditions

### Our Shared Assumptions

**Foundational commitments that guide our work:**

<details class="toggle">
<summary><strong>The Primacy of Tacit Knowledge & Practice</strong></summary>
<div class="body">

Engaging in the world through practice (meditation, prayer, technology) is ontologically fundamental, not secondary to theory.

</div>
</details>

<details class="toggle">
<summary><strong>Science as a Mode of Revealing</strong></summary>
<div class="body">

A valid explanation is not just a model, but a tool to enhance our perception of the world, where scientific analysis acts as a "linguistic cue" to deeper insight

</div>
</details>

<details class="toggle">
<summary><strong>The Insufficiency of Reductionism</strong></summary>
<div class="body">

Phenomena cannot be fully explained by their lower-level components alone; reductionism is necessary but insufficient.

</div>
</details>

<details class="toggle">
<summary><strong>Relational Ontology</strong></summary>
<div class="body">

Interactions and relationships are just as fundamental as the components themselves, necessitating top-down explanation where parts are explained by the whole.

</div>
</details>

<details class="toggle">
<summary><strong>A "Seed-Grow-Harvest" Epistemology</strong></summary>
<div class="body">

Critical reflection on the process of research, going beyond mere data acquisition and generalization. In general, recognizing a cycle from an initial spontaneous insight through analytic unfolding, culminating in a mature fruit that can be shared, and yields new seeds.

</div>
</details>

</div>
