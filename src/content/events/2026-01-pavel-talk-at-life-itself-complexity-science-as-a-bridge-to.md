Complexity Science as a bridge to Eastern Philosophy

Modern mindfulness research often dilutes the ancient practice of sati, stripping away its deep ethical and philosophical roots to fit a reductionist scientific mold. This talk proposes Complexity Science as the authentic bridge to recover that lost wisdom. We will explore how rigorous concepts like emergence and feedback loops mirror Buddhist insights into "dependent origination" and the fluid nature of the self. By shifting our lens from simple linear cause-and-effect to complex interconnectedness, we can scientifically validate Eastern worldviews and restore the transformative power that Western mindfulness left behind.
