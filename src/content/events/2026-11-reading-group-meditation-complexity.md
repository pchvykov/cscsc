We read *Meditation and complexity: a review and synthesis of evidence* by Daniel Atad, Pedro Mediano, Fernando Rosas and Aviva Berkovich-Ohana (*Neuroscience of Consciousness*, 2025) - [open access here](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niaf013). It reviews what we know about the complexity of brain activity in meditation: short-term effects during practice, longer-term changes in regular meditators, and how these differ across styles of practice, and it proposes a framework for making sense of the findings. Hosted by Matthew McCarthy.
