- \*Where can the most impactful science be tackled today?\*\*

The answer is no longer strictly the university. In fact, traditional academic silos often penalize the very interdisciplinary, "misfit" thinking required to solve today's complex problems. As a result, the "structural misfits" of the Ivory Tower are increasingly finding homes in a new ecosystem of deep-tech VCs, Focused Research Organizations (FROs), and corporate labs.

The trend seems clear -- the center of gravity for the most interesting research is drifting away from the tenure track. This interactive session discusses how to navigate this "vibe shift," much of which is driven by the AI revolution. We will map these emerging organizations and debate how to align a career path - or a developing research community - with the changing realities of scientific discovery.
