How do we live in a world that is beyond our comprehension - how do we act and make decisions when we know we can't capture it? Living Questions is a sharing and listening circle that grew out of the Mindful Researchers community. This one used a "drops and ripples" format: a few short seeds - a concrete example and a little theory - each followed by quiet circling, where people speak when moved, offer a few sentences, and let it settle before the next voice.
