Organizer: Matthieu Barbier<br>Time: Fri Dec 12 · 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM GMT+5:30<br>Meeting link (Jitsi): [https://meet.jit.si/CSCSCconnect](https://meet.jit.si/CSCSCconnect)

The goal of this group is to work together on a collaborative research project, with the following aim: inventing a basic formal language to express what it means to play a role in a system across diverse sciences (a way to mathematically model the many scientific explanations, whether in biology, sociology, linguistics, engineering... that are not anchored in reduction to / emergence from smaller components, but instead in participation to / utility for larger wholes).

This question has been around for a good while, and yet some essential pitfalls have prevented the invention of a "telic" (goal, purpose, role, meaning... oriented) equivalent of abstract languages such as differential calculus, that are shared by many different fields and thus allow transfers of intuitions and techniques between them.
