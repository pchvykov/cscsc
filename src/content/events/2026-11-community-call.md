Hagar Tal is first author of *[Active Inference, Computational Phenomenology, and Advanced Meditation: Toward the Formalization of the Experience of Meditation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41475512)* (with Malcolm Wright, Shawn Prest, Lars Sandved-Smith and Matthew D. Sacchet). She talks through the process of writing it and the big ideas that came out - across the computational models of deep meditative states, the place they converge is precision weighting: shifts in experience as shifts in how much the mind trusts its own predictions. The call is a conversation more than a presentation, and no background in active inference is needed.
