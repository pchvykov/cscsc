Title: Proper Nesting

Author: Iuval Clejan

Abstract: I investigate the question "why do multicellular organisms have organelles, cells and organs instead of just a soup of organic molecules, and is this multi-level formation a general pattern of complex systems, generalizable to social, brain/cognitive, and computational systems?"

Some hypotheses and simulations follow. The main 2 hypotheses are that

1. Bottom-up: The benefits of specialization lead to interacting modules, but the costs of coordination, monitoring for, and incentivizing alignment and punishing and fixing/healing misalignment can be reduced with vertical level emergence
2. Top-down: Some complex tasks can be done better by delegating simpler sub-tasks to specialized modules, which can do the same for sub-modules and sub-sub-tasks, in a recursive way.
